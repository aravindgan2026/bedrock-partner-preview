1Who you can share with

  • Share only with people you know personally.
  • Only accredited investors may invest. If you are not sure someone is accredited, do not share it with them.
  • No public posts, ads or mass messages.

2What you may say

  • Say only what is on the deal page.
  • Never promise returns.
  • Never call it guaranteed, and never say it is registered with any regulator.

3Partner fee

  • Your fee is 6% of the money that comes in through your page, shown on your tracker.
  • The #1 partner by shares sold on a deal earns 9% on that deal (draft).
  • It is paid after the investor's money is in.
  • The fee may change from deal to deal. Each deal shows its own fee.

4Your brand on the page

  • You allow your logo and name to be shown on your page.
  • Bedrock remains the sponsor of the offering and is shown in the page footer.
