Questions people ask

How does it work?
  1. Sign up and get your own page for this deal, with your name and logo.
  2. Send it to people you know, one at a time.
  3. Earn a fee when they invest. Your partner portal shows visits, holds and money in.
How much do I earn, and when?
6% of what your people put in. The top partner on each deal earns 9%. It comes out of Bedrock’s own fee, never out of your people’s money. It is paid after your people’s money is in (signed and funded). We use the phone or email you give us to reach you and pay you.
What do my clients buy?
Whole shares in one house flip in the Orlando area. They tap “Hold my spot” on your page, and our team follows up with each one.
Who can invest?
Only accredited investors. Share only with people you know personally.
Do I need a license?
Check your own license and follow your own firm’s rules before you share. That part is on you.
Can I post it on social media?
No. No public posts, ads or mass messages. Send it one to one.
Can I bring on other pros?
Yes. Your partner portal gives you an invite link. You choose how much of their fee you take, and their sales count toward your rank.
I lost my private link

Tell us your company and how to reach you. Our team checks every day, makes sure it is you, and sends you a new link.
